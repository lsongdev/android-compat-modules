# Bluetooth compatibility

Verified on BOE Funbook C1 (C1S100), MT8788 / Mali-G72 MP3, Android 10 vendor stack, unofficial LineageOS 21 / Android 14, Magisk 31.1.

## Verified 1.1.0 behavior — 2026-11-15

Version 1.1.0 reached ON after reboot and off/on cycling without the previous startup abort. The command override remained 182. Peripheral pairing and audio were not tested; startup success does not establish profile compatibility.
